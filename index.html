Chapter One
The Day Everything Changed
✨

Some moments split time in two — there is the before, and then there is the after. The day you became pregnant, was one of those moments.

I remember the way your eyes held a universe of emotions — joy and fear and wonder, all swirling together like the most beautiful storm. In that single moment, our world expanded to make room for someone we hadn't met yet, but already loved more than words could hold.

Two lines on a test. A heartbeat on a screen. And just like that, we were no longer two — we were three.

Chapter Two
The Secret Garden
🌱

These first weeks have been our secret — a quiet miracle growing beneath your heart. A tiny seed, no bigger than a sesame seed (حبة السمسماية), already rewriting every plan we'd made.

But the secret garden has its thorns. The days that you battled -and still battling- with nausea. The mornings & afternoons when exhaustion wrapped around you like a heavy blanket. The evenings when you'd look at me with tired eyes and say, "I just want to feel normal again."

And I wished I could carry it for you, Sondos. Every wave of nausea, every moment of tiredness. But all I could do was hold your hand, bring you water, and remind you — you are growing a whole human being. That is the furthest thing from normal. That is extraordinary.

We've made it through the hardest part, my love. The first chapter is closing. And you were so brave — even on the days you didn't feel brave at all.

Chapter Three
The Bloom That's Coming
🌺

Just ahead of us lies the bloom — that golden stretch of the second trimester when your body will finally say, "I've got this." The nausea will fade like a bad dream, and in its place will come something miraculous: energy.

Your belly will begin to show — a gentle curve that will make my heart swell every time I see it. People say pregnant women glow, but with you it will be different. There's already a light in you, Sondos — and soon it will have even more reason to shine.

And then — the moment I'm waiting for — the first flutter. A tiny kick, like a small hello from the inside. Our baby, saying "I'm here, and I'm already strong."

I can't wait for that moment. I can't wait to feel our baby move for the first time.

Chapter Four
The Long Goodnight Ahead
🌙

Then will come the third trimester — a long, deep breath before the biggest moment of our lives. Your belly will be round and proud — Full of life — and every movement inside will be a reminder that our baby is running out of room and getting ready.

But the long goodnight won't be without its trials. Sleep will become a distant memory, replaced by a nightly game of finding the one comfortable position — which will last approximately ten minutes before the baby decides to practice gymnastics.

Your feet will ache. Your back will protest. Your heart will burn with an intensity that has nothing to do with emotion and everything to do with acid reflux. And through it all, Sondos, I know you'll keep going.

And when those hard nights come, I'll be right there. By your side helping you & reminding you that every discomfort is a countdown to the most beautiful moment of our lives.

Chapter Five
A Letter to You
My dearest Sondos,

I need you to know something, and I need you to really hear it: you are the most beloved person to my heart.

I have watched you carry this child with a grace that leaves me speechless. Through the nausea and the tears, through the exhaustion and the anxious thoughts, through the body that changed in ways you didn't prepare for — you never once stopped being extraordinary.

And we're just getting started. There are harder days ahead — I won't pretend there aren't. But there are also days coming that will take your breath away. The first kick. The first time we see our baby's face on a screen. The first time you hold your belly and laugh because someone is having a party in there.

There will be days you won't feel beautiful. But Sondos, you have never been more beautiful than you are right now. Every change in your body is a love letter written to our child. Every sacrifice is a down payment on a lifetime of care.

Our baby doesn't know it yet, but they have already won the lottery of life — because they get you as a mother. And I get to walk every step of this road beside you.

Thank you for agreeing to go through this journey. I promise to spend every day by your side — through the easy days and the hard ones, through the sleepless nights and the first smiles, through it all.

Forever yours,
Omer

The Day We're Waiting For

Someday soon — and sooner than we think — the day will come. It will arrive like all life-changing days do: unexpectedly, impossibly, and with a force that rearranges everything.

You will be magnificent, Sondos. Through every contraction, through every moment that pushes you to the edge of what you think you can endure, you will find more. More strength. More courage. More love. You'll reach deep into a place you didn't know existed and pull out a kind of power that I will spend the rest of my life in awe of.

And then — a cry. The most beautiful, earth-shattering, world-rebuilding cry we'll ever hear. Our baby will be here. Our baby will be HERE.

They'll place that tiny, perfect being on your chest, and the whole universe will narrow to the space between your heartbeat and theirs. Two hearts that have been sharing one body, finally meeting face to face.

And in that moment, every difficult day, every sleepless night, every moment of fear and doubt — it will all make sense. It will all be worth it. It will all have been leading here.

That day is coming, Sondos. And until it does, I will be by your side through every step, every kick, every tear, and every smile. Because this journey isn't just yours — it's ours. And the best is yet to come.
